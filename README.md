# AI Expense Tracker

> 🥈 **2nd Place — IBM Hackathon, July 15, 2026**

An AI-powered expense management platform built using **IBM Bob** and **IBM watsonx.ai** that transforms uploaded receipts and expenses into understandable, personalized financial insights.

The project was originally developed around automating expense processing and financial analysis. I expanded the platform with a focus on **financial accessibility, personalization, and long-term expense tracking**, helping users understand not just where their money is going, but what their financial results actually mean.

## ✨ Features

### 📄 AI Expense Processing

Users can upload expenses and receipts, allowing the application to automatically process and organize financial information.

The AI can:

- Extract expense information from uploaded documents
- Categorize purchases
- Analyze spending
- Generate financial summaries
- Visualize expense data

---

### 🧠 Beginner Mode

Financial information can be difficult to understand when reports contain unfamiliar terminology.

**Beginner Mode** translates financial jargon into clear, everyday language so users without a financial background can still understand their results.

For example:

**Standard**

> Your discretionary expenditure increased significantly compared with the previous reporting period.

**Beginner Mode**

> You spent more money this period on things you chose to buy, such as entertainment, eating out, or shopping.

This makes financial insights accessible to a much wider range of users.

---

### 👤 Personalized User Modes

Users can change their **user type**, allowing the application to adapt how financial results are presented.

Instead of providing the same explanation to everyone, the AI translates and contextualizes results based on the selected user's needs.

This allows financial insights to become more relevant and easier to understand depending on who is using the platform.

---

### 📊 Weekly & Monthly Expense Tracking

Uploaded expenses are automatically incorporated into an ongoing expense history.

Users can switch between:

- 📅 **Weekly spending**
- 🗓️ **Monthly spending**

This allows users to monitor how their spending changes over time instead of analyzing every uploaded expense independently.

Users can quickly understand:

- How much they are spending
- Where their money is going
- Changes in spending over time
- Which categories contribute most to their expenses
